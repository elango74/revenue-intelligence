One Decision Deliberately Rejected

Alternative Considered: Summing currency figures extracted from transcripts (INR 10–13 lakh, INR 26–39 lakh, INR 1.3 crore, EUR 2,600–6,500, USD 260, INR 18,200) into a headline "Total Pipeline Value (₹ / $)" KPI card and ranking reps and meeting organisers on a single unified sales leaderboard.

Why We Rejected It:

  1. Incompatible Units & Business Meanings: The numbers spoken in transcripts are not comparable deal values — EUR 2,600–6,500 in M02 (Turn 31) is a supplier setup cost SpendNest would pay; INR 1.3 crore in M04 (Turn 22) is a customer's existing bank credit limit; INR 18,200 in M07 (Turn 21) is a UI bug reproduction amount; and 390–520 invoices/mo in M06 (Turn 16) is explicitly caveated as a rough conversational guess.
  2. Role & Sample Boundaries: Calendar organizer_email does not prove selling ownership or attendance, and the 44-record package is a curated variety extract rather than a full-team performance sample.

Trade-Off Accepted & Resulting Product Choice: We traded away a single aggregated pipeline-dollar chart and unified rep leaderboard in favour of two separate activity tables on the Manager Dashboard (Call Reps by rep_email vs. Meeting Organisers by organizer_email) with explicit denominators, while surfacing commercial figures strictly inside each conversation's contextual evidence card.
